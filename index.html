Questions worth asking
before you migrate.

We are certified PCI DSS Level 1 and SOC 2 Type II, audited annually. Card details never touch your servers: they are tokenized in the browser and stored in a hardware-backed vault, so your PCI scope stays at SAQ-A. Everything in transit uses TLS 1.3, and every field at rest is encrypted with keys rotated on a 90-day cycle.

The rate on the plan, and nothing else. No setup fee, no monthly minimum, no charge for failed or declined payments, and no fee to open extra currency balances. Cross-border and currency conversion are quoted up front at interbank plus 0.2%. Chargebacks cost $15, refunded to you if you win the dispute.

Hosted checkout is a single API call and a redirect — most teams are taking test payments within an hour. A custom flow with our SDKs typically takes two to three days. If you are moving from another provider we will migrate your stored cards and customer records for you, usually inside a week and with no re-authentication for your customers.

All major card networks, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, and more than 60 local methods including Pix, UPI, iDEAL, Bancontact, BLIK, SEPA Direct Debit, ACH and M-Pesa. You can charge in 135 currencies and settle into any of 42. Meridian picks the methods to display based on the shopper's country, device and basket size.

On a rolling two-day schedule by default, including weekends in supported markets. Growth and Enterprise accounts can move to same-day or on-demand payouts. Every payout arrives with a reconciliation file that maps each deposit line back to the individual payments, refunds and fees inside it.

Every account gets documentation, the sandbox and email support answered by engineers, not a script. Growth adds a shared Slack channel with a one-hour response target during business hours. Enterprise adds 24/7 paging, a named solutions engineer and quarterly reviews of your authorization rates.
